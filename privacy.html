Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you use the booking, contact or application forms, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, preferred date, location and project or application details.

How we use it

We use this information only to reply to your enquiry, plan and deliver your project, and send quotations or receipts. We do not sell or share your details with third parties.

Team and training applications

If you apply through the Join the Team page, we receive the details you enter (such as your name, contact details, skills and motivation). We use them only to review your application, contact you and, if you are selected, arrange assessment, training or onboarding. We do not make applications public and we will delete them on request.

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to our email through the FormSubmit service, which processes them on our behalf.

Your photos and media

Photos, videos and audio from your project are delivered to you privately. We will ask for your permission before featuring your project in our portfolio or on social media.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing jcreativesmultimediaproduction@gmail.com.
