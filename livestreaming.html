Livestreaming

Live coverage of your service, conference or event.

Book a Project Chat on WhatsApp

What this covers

Livestreaming involves much more than a camera. We help you plan and run a live broadcast so people who cannot attend can still be part of your event.

Typical work

  • Church services and programmes
  • Conferences, seminars and graduations
  • Weddings and family events
  • Corporate and institutional events

Possible deliverables

  • A live stream to your chosen platform, where supported
  • A recording of the programme
  • Optional edited highlights afterwards, if agreed

What we need from you

  • Venue and event schedule
  • The platform you want to stream to (for example YouTube or Facebook)
  • Number of cameras and the audio setup at the venue
  • Internet availability at the venue

Good to know

A live production can involve video capture, audio capture, mixing, signal routing, camera switching, streaming software, internet connectivity, monitoring and recording. Equipment, crew size and internet needs depend on your event. Tell us the details in your enquiry and we will confirm what we can cover and what must be arranged in advance. A stable internet connection at the venue is essential, so we discuss this early.

Submitting an enquiry is not a confirmed booking. We reply with availability and a quotation, and your date is only reserved once we both confirm.
