Photography

Book a Project Chat on WhatsApp

What this covers

From studio portraits to event coverage, we photograph people, occasions and brands with attention to light, composition and detail.

Typical work

  • Studio and outdoor portraits
  • Weddings, birthdays and family occasions
  • Church, school and conference events
  • Brand and product photography

Possible deliverables

  • Edited digital photographs
  • A selection of highlights for quick sharing
  • Online delivery of your final images
  • Prints, photobooks and framed photos on request

What we need from you

  • Date, time and location
  • Purpose of the shoot (personal, business or event)
  • Preferred style or reference images
  • Number of people, or hours of coverage needed

Good to know

Delivery time depends on the size of the project and is agreed when your booking is confirmed.

Submitting an enquiry is not a confirmed booking. We reply with availability and a quotation, and your date is only reserved once we both confirm.
